Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. This policy describes what information Khrystyna Skotte Photography ("I", "me") collects on khrystynaskotte.com and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you submit the inquiry form, you share details such as your name, email address, phone number, event date, venue, and message. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and plan your session or event. Form submissions are delivered to my email via FormSubmit, and an automatic confirmation is sent to the email address you provide. I never sell your information.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, time on page, general region). These tools use cookies and collect usage data in aggregate; they do not identify you personally to me.

Advertising

I advertise on Google. Google Ads conversion tracking uses cookies to measure whether visitors who click an ad later inquire, and remarketing audiences may be used to show ads to previous visitors. If you submit the inquiry form, your email address and phone number may be shared with Google in hashed (unreadable) form solely to measure advertising effectiveness ("enhanced conversions"). You can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com.

Scheduling and payments

Consultation calls are scheduled through Calendly, which has its own privacy policy. Any payment details are handled by the payment provider named in your contract. This site does not collect payment information.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the information I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, at any time. Contact me at khrystynaskotte@gmail.com or (619) 577-6091.
