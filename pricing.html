The guest-count addition is a flat $1,000 whether you host 61 or 200 guests. It covers a second photographer and expanded coverage for larger celebrations, and it applies once per booking, not once per service, even when you book photography and videography a la carte. Want a second photographer for a wedding under 60 guests? It's available as an add-on to any wedding package for $150 per hour, capped at $1,000, just mention it when you inquire.

Not sure how many hours you need? As a guide: 4 hours usually covers the ceremony, portraits, and the start of the reception. 6 hours runs from before the ceremony through cake cutting and open dancing. 8 hours covers getting ready in the morning all the way through your send-off. Coverage starts when Khrystyna arrives at your venue, so travel time never counts against your hours.

Add-ons, priced simply: extra photographer $150 per hour (capped at $1,000), drone coverage $300, heirloom album from $250, rush delivery $400 for weddings or $200 for sessions.

Every collection is delivered digitally through your private online gallery, so there is no sales tax on your package. California sales tax applies only to physical keepsakes like albums, and any tax is stated in your written quote before you sign.

No Surprises

How Booking Works

Every booking is covered by a signed contract, so you know exactly what is reserved and what is owed before anything is due.

1. Check your date

Send an inquiry or book a free 30 minute call. I confirm availability and answer questions before you commit to anything.

2. Sign and reserve

A signed contract plus a 50% retainer reserves your date. The remaining balance is due 14 days before your wedding or session.

3. Plan together

We build your timeline, walk through locations and permits, and coordinate with your planner or venue as the day gets closer.

4. Receive your gallery

Every gallery is delivered within 2 to 3 weeks in a private online gallery, weddings and sessions alike.

Plans change. You may reschedule once with at least 30 days' notice and your retainer carries over. Outdoor sessions affected by unsafe weather are rescheduled at no extra charge. See booking and cancellation questions answered. Full terms are in the contract you receive before any payment is due.
