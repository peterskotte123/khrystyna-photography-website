Good to Know

How the County Clerk Ceremony Works

Three things couples are surprised by. Details come from the County Clerk's page and can change, so confirm when you book. Full walkthrough: how to get married at the San Diego courthouse, step by step.

Indoor room or the outdoor arbor

Downtown offers both. Indoor ceremonies hold 15 to 20 guests depending on the office. Outdoor ceremonies have no guest limit and the best light.

No petals, confetti, or champagne on site

The Clerk does not allow decor, tossing, or alcohol at its ceremony areas. We do the toast and the petals afterward, a short walk away, and it photographs better anyway.

Any office in the county

Downtown on Pacific Highway, or the branch offices in San Marcos, Santee, and Chula Vista. Same price, no travel fee, and we pick a portrait spot nearby.

Questions

Before You Text

How soon can you shoot? Our ceremony is next week.

Text me the date and time. Weekday ceremonies are often easier to fit in, and before 3pm you get a same-day answer. A $325 retainer holds the date.

What if the Clerk runs behind?

It happens. I plan the portraits around your appointment, not the other way around, so a wait at the window does not swallow your photos.

Can you be our witness?

Yes, and couples ask all the time. A public marriage license needs one witness who is 18 or older, and I am happy to sign as yours at no charge. I am there anyway. If you have a confidential license, no witness is needed at all.

Can our parents and friends come?

Yes. Up to 15 guests are welcome at any coverage length, and I make sure there are photos with everyone who came. If your group is bigger than 15, it is $100 more and everyone is welcome.

Where do the portraits happen downtown?

The waterfront park beside the County Administration Center and the harbor are a short walk from the door, and Little Italy is close by. With a second hour we can drive to Balboa Park or the coast.

We are awkward in front of a camera.

Most couples say that. I give you small things to do, walk here, fix his tie, read your vows again, and the photos come from the moments in between. Nobody has to pose.

What should we wear?

Whatever you would wear to a nice dinner, or the dress. Light and solid colors read well against the waterfront. Bring comfortable shoes for the walk.

We have not booked the Clerk yet. How does that work?

Ceremonies are by appointment only, up to 7 weeks out, and dates fill fast. You can get the license and marry in the same visit by booking a "License and Ceremony" appointment. Book that first with the step-by-step guide, then text me the time and I hold it on my side. County fees are separate from photography.

We have our own officiant. Does the same price apply?

Yes. A park, a backyard, a restaurant, a friend officiating: the same one hour coverage applies anywhere in San Diego County.

Do you also film?

Yes. Photo and film together is $1,350 for one hour of each. I capture one and a trusted second shooter covers the other, so nothing is missed.

When do we get the photos?

Your private online gallery arrives in 2 to 3 weeks with 100+ edited photos. Rush delivery in 3 to 4 days is available if you need them for an announcement.
