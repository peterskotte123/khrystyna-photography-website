Timing

When to Book

The window most moms love is weeks 28 to 34: the bump is unmistakable and you still feel like walking a beach. Earlier or later works too, so text me and we will find your week.

Weeks 28 to 34

The classic window. Book four to six weeks ahead so the date you want is open.

Weeks 35 and up

Still beautiful, just closer to the car. We pick a spot with a short walk and a place to sit.

Sooner than that

Twins, a planned early delivery, or you just feel great now. Say so and we go earlier.

Questions

Before You Text

I do not feel like being photographed right now.

Most moms say that at the start, and most say the opposite when the gallery arrives. I do not pose you. I give you small things to do and talk the whole time, and we sit whenever you want to. The photos above are all from sessions that started with "I am not sure about this."

What should I wear?

Something that fits the bump and feels good: a long flowing dress, a fitted knit, a crop top and jeans, or a lace piece if that is you. Soft colors that do not fight the sunset: cream, sage, sand, dusty blue. I send a short guide once you book, and you can text me photos of options.

Can my partner and our kids be in it?

Yes, and the dog too. There is no extra charge for anyone who lives with you. We do photos of just you, the two of you, and everyone together, and the kids can wander off when they have had enough.

What time of day?

The last ninety minutes before sunset, almost always. Morning works if you are an early person or the coast is socked in. I check the light and the tide for your date before we lock the spot.

What if the baby comes early, or the weather turns?

We move the date, no charge, and your retainer moves with it. Marine layer is soft, even light, so we usually shoot through it. Rain or real wind reschedules.

Do you have gowns I can borrow?

No, and I think your own clothes photograph better anyway. If you want something flowing for the session, a long dress from your closet or a rental works, and I will tell you what will move well in the wind.

Can we do photos once the baby is here?

Yes. A family session is the same hour at the same price, at home or at the beach, whenever you are ready. Mention it when you text and I will keep the dates in mind.

How soon can we book?

Text me your due date and you get an answer the same day if it is before 3pm. Weekday sunsets are easier to find than weekends, so if your schedule is flexible, say so.
