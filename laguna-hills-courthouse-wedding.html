Good to Know

How the Laguna Hills Ceremony Works

The office is the Clerk-Recorder's South County Branch in the Laguna Hills Civic Center, 24031 El Toro Road. Details come from the Orange County Clerk-Recorder's page and can change, so confirm when you book.

Appointments are online only

The county books civil ceremonies through its website, not by phone or walk-in. Its ceremony fee is $28, separate from photography.

Twenty guests, one witness

Up to 20 guests can attend. A public license needs one witness and the county does not provide one. I can sign as yours.

An Orange County license

The office marries couples whose license was issued by the Orange County Clerk-Recorder, and a county deputy clerk performs the ceremony.

Questions

Before You Text

How soon can you shoot? Our ceremony is next week.

Text me the date and time. Weekday ceremonies are often easier to fit in, and before 3pm Pacific you get a same-day answer. A $425 retainer holds the date.

What if the Clerk runs behind?

It happens. I plan the portraits around your appointment, not the other way around, so a wait at the window does not swallow your photos.

Can you be our witness?

Yes, and couples ask all the time. A public marriage license needs one witness, the county does not provide one, and I am happy to sign as yours at no charge. I am there anyway.

Can our parents and friends come?

Yes. Up to 15 guests are welcome at any coverage length, and I make sure there are photos with everyone who came. For 16 to 20 guests it is $100 more. Twenty is the county limit for the ceremony.

Where do the portraits happen?

Around the Civic Center right after the ceremony. With a second hour we drive to the coast: Laguna Beach is about 20 minutes away.

We are awkward in front of a camera.

Most couples say that. I give you small things to do, walk here, fix his tie, read your vows again, and the photos come from the moments in between. Nobody has to pose.

What should we wear?

Whatever you would wear to a nice dinner, or the dress. Light and solid colors read well.

We have not booked the Clerk yet. How does that work?

Ceremonies are by appointment only, booked on the county website. Book that first on the Clerk-Recorder's civil ceremony page, then text me the time and I hold it on my side. County fees are separate from photography.

Are you local to Orange County?

I am based in San Diego and drive up for Laguna Hills ceremonies. The price already includes travel, so there is nothing to add.

We have our own officiant. Does the same price apply?

Yes. A park, a beach, a backyard, a friend officiating: the same pricing applies around Laguna Hills, Laguna Beach, Dana Point and San Clemente.

Do you also film?

Yes. Photo and film together is $1,850 for one hour of each. I capture one and a trusted second shooter covers the other, so nothing is missed.

When do we get the photos?

Your private online gallery arrives in 2 to 3 weeks with 100+ edited photos. Rush delivery in 3 to 4 days is available if you need them for an announcement.
