Questions

Before You Text

Is there really no travel fee to Temecula?

None. Temecula Valley and Fallbrook are priced the same as San Diego. For venues farther out, you get one flat travel cost in writing within 24 hours of your inquiry, and it never changes after booking.

What time should a vineyard ceremony start?

Late enough that the portraits land in the last hour of light. Once you have a date, I map sunset for that day and we work backward from there. Summer days in the valley run hot, so the heavy posing happens in shade or at golden hour, never at 2pm on the lawn.

How many hours do we need?

Four hours covers the ceremony, portraits, and the start of the reception. Six runs from before the ceremony through cake cutting and open dancing. Eight is the full day, from getting ready through the send-off. We can settle this once you have a rough timeline.

We have more than 60 guests.

Over 60 guests adds $1,000 to the Basic, Gold, or Premium collection and brings a second photographer, so nothing at a bigger wedding gets missed. The Signature Collection already includes the second photographer with no guest-count addition.

Can we get a film as well?

Yes. The Signature Collection is photo and cinematic film together for $5,000: a highlight film plus the full ceremony and speeches edit, with a second shooter so both are captured at once.

When do we get the photos?

Your private online gallery arrives in 2 to 3 weeks, and every photo is color-edited with light, natural retouching. Rush delivery is available for an additional charge.

How do we hold the date?

A 50% retainer reserves it, with the balance due later. Text me your date and venue first and you get a same-day answer on availability before 3pm.
