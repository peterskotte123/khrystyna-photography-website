Weddings

From Getting Ready to the Last Dance

A wedding day moves quickly. One moment you are pinning a boutonniere with shaking hands, and a few hours later your grandmother is on the dance floor. My job is to stay close enough to catch all of it and quiet enough that you forget I am there.

I photograph weddings across San Diego: hotel and resort ballrooms, coastal clubs, garden venues, and backyard celebrations at home. Whether you are hosting 40 guests or 200, the coverage is the same in spirit. I arrive early, I know the timeline because we built it together, and I am still shooting when the sparklers come out.

Every collection includes light, natural skin retouching on every image, support through planning and on the day itself, and a private online gallery you can share with your family.

Where You Celebrate

Every Kind of San Diego Wedding

Hotels & Resorts

Ballrooms, terraces, and resort lawns. Hosting somewhere like Hotel del Coronado? The Signature Collection is built for venue weddings, with a second photographer already included.

Wineries & Vineyards

Vineyard ceremonies and barrel-room receptions, including the Temecula Valley. Wine country weddings are covered by the same collections, with no travel fee inside San Diego County or Temecula. Venues beyond that get an exact travel cost in writing within 24 hours of your inquiry, one flat line item that never changes after booking.

Garden & Backyard

Intimate celebrations at home or in a garden venue. Smaller guest counts get the same full attention, and the Basic and Gold collections are made for exactly this. Want film too? The Signature Collection covers garden weddings at the same flat $5,000.

Private Estates

Full-property, multi-location celebrations in Rancho Santa Fe, Del Mar, and La Jolla are handled through the Estate Collection, quoted individually with complete discretion.
