About Me

The story behind the lens

★★★★★  30 five-star reviews on Thumbtack · Top Pro

Hi, I'm Khrystyna

I'm a San Diego wedding photographer and filmmaker. Weddings are the heart of my work, from barefoot elopements on the cliffs at Cardiff and Sunset Cliffs, to hotel and resort celebrations at venues like Hotel del Coronado, to private estate weddings in Rancho Santa Fe, Del Mar, and La Jolla. Photography has always been more than just a career for me. It's my way of seeing the world and connecting with the people I photograph.

What I love most about photography is noticing the little details, the way light falls on a face, a genuine smile, an unguarded moment between two people. I see photography as a mix of storytelling and artistry, and my goal is to create images that feel both authentic and timeless.

My approach is simple: I want you to feel comfortable, celebrated, and truly yourself in front of the camera. Whether we're capturing a full wedding day from getting ready to the last dance, the quiet intimacy of an elopement, or a surprise proposal I've helped you plan down to the minute, I'm here to make the experience feel natural and enjoyable. I also photograph portraits, families, and maternity sessions, and many couples first find me long before their wedding day.

I believe photography should be an experience, not just a product. When you work with me, you're not just getting beautiful images; you're getting someone who genuinely cares about your story and wants to tell it in the most heartfelt way possible.
