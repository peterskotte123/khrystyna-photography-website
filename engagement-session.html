Where

Four Spots I Keep Coming Back To

All free, all within the county, all best in the last hour of light. Have your own place in mind? Even better.

Sunset Cliffs

Tide pools, sandstone, and the sun going straight into the water. The classic for a reason.

La Jolla and Windansea

Bluffs above the surf, a beach shack, and softer light under the marine layer.

Torrey Pines and Black's Beach

Long walks on empty sand below the cliffs. Bring a second outfit for the water.

Balboa Park

Arches, gardens, and the Spanish buildings for a dressed-up session that does not need a beach.

Questions

Before You Text

We are awkward in front of a camera.

Nearly everyone says that at the start. I do not pose you. I give you small things to do and talk the whole time, and most couples forget the camera by the second spot. The photos above are all from couples who said the same thing.

What should we wear?

Something you feel good in, in soft colors that do not fight the sunset: cream, sage, sand, rust, denim. Long dresses move beautifully in the wind. Avoid big logos and matching outfits. I send a short guide once you book, and you can text me photos of options.

What time of day?

The last ninety minutes before sunset, almost always. Morning sessions work too if the coast is socked in, or if you want the beach empty. I check the light and the tide for your date before we lock the spot.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes, and I hope you do. Bring a leash and a friend to hold it between shots, and tell me when you book so I pick a spot where dogs are allowed.

How soon can we book?

Text me a date and you get an answer the same day if it is before 3pm. Weekday sunsets are easier to find than weekends, so if your schedule is flexible, say so.

Can we use the photos for save-the-dates and our wedding website?

Yes. Your gallery downloads in full resolution and has a share link for family, so you can order cards and prints from it or anywhere you like.

What if the weather turns?

Marine layer is soft, even light, so we usually shoot through it. Rain or wind that makes the day miserable reschedules once at no charge, and your retainer moves with the date.

Do you shoot weddings too?

Yes, and many couples book both. The Estate collection on the pricing page includes a complimentary engagement session, and any other collection can add one. Ask when you text.
