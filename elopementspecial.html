Booking Is Simple

Every booking is covered by a signed contract. A 50% retainer reserves your date and the remaining balance is due 14 days before your day. Every gallery is delivered within 2 to 3 weeks, and you may reschedule once with at least 30 days' notice. If unsafe weather moves in, rescheduling is free and your retainer carries over to the new date. If you cancel outright, the retainer is non-refundable, since it holds your date. Coverage starts when Khrystyna arrives, so travel time never counts against your hours.
