Good to Know

Proposal Questions

Will the surprise stay a surprise?

Yes. We plan everything over text and email so nothing appears where it shouldn't, the setup is finished before you arrive, and I photograph from a distance with a long lens, looking like anyone else enjoying the view, until the moment happens.

What does it cost all together?

Here is the honest math:

  • Photography only: $650, exact.
  • Picnic proposal (setup + photography): most land between $1,400 and $2,000, before tax and any permit.
  • Photo + film with the setup: typically $2,900 to $3,045.
  • On top of any setup: about 7.75% California sales tax on the Hidden Picnics portion, plus about $25 to $100 if your spot needs a park permit.

Proposal setups cost more than plain picnics because they include the romantic staging itself, the candles, florals, and decor, plus the coordination of keeping it all a secret. Photos are delivered within 2 to 3 weeks. The film is a cinematic highlight of about a minute, plus vertical clips for sharing, delivered on the same timeline. Live audio of the moment, rush delivery, and same-week sneak peeks can all be added, with each fee quoted upfront. The estimator above shows your exact combination.

Where can we do it?

  • Sunset Cliffs: dramatic bluffs over open ocean, the most requested sunset spot. No permit for photography only; styled setups need one.
  • La Jolla: coves, sea cliffs, and bluff-top lawns above the water.
  • Coronado: wide, quiet sand with the skyline across the bay.
  • Mission Bay: calm water, grassy parks, and easy parking.

Anywhere in San Diego County works, with no photography travel fee inside the county.

Visiting San Diego?

Everything is planned remotely over text, email, and a free call, from the exact spot to the timing of the light, so you just land and show up. You do not need to pick a spot before you reach out: tell me your dates and I will send you two or three options with timing and parking, and one call sorts the rest.

What about weather?

If unsafe weather moves in, my photography reschedules at no charge. Hidden Picnics watches the forecast too, and their policy is to reschedule the setup to a future date when the rain chance reaches 40% or more, with indoor alternatives offered where possible. Your date moves, and we coordinate the new plan together, one conversation, not two. Traveling in with a fixed window, or a date that cannot move? Say so upfront and we build a backup slot and a rain plan into the schedule from the start.

How does booking and payment work?

One inquiry starts everything, and I stay in the middle so both sides move together. Photography is covered by my contract with a 50% retainer. If you add a styled scene, Hidden Picnics sends their own agreement with a 50% deposit, with their balance due 72 hours before the event. Both deposits hold your date, and weather reschedules move everything to your new date. If plans shift, you can reschedule once with 30 days' notice, and a surprise that has to move on shorter notice gets my best effort to move with it. The deposits themselves are non-refundable if you cancel outright, and if I ever have to cancel on you, you get a full refund.

Do we need a permit?

Photography only, no setup? No permit, and that includes Sunset Cliffs. In the rare case your exact spot is an exception, I flag it while we plan and handle the paperwork for you. Adding a styled setup? That needs a park use permit in San Diego public parks, including Sunset Cliffs, which sits on the approved city shoreline park list. We can handle the whole permit process for you, with the fee quoted upfront, so you never have to deal with the city. Most spots need about 15 days of lead time, and the most popular shoreline parks can need more, so the earlier you inquire the more spots stay open.
