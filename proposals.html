Good to Know

Proposal Questions

Will the surprise stay a surprise?

Yes. We plan everything over text and email so nothing appears where it shouldn't, and I photograph from a distance with a long lens, looking like anyone else enjoying the view, until the moment happens.

What does it cost all together?

  • Photography: $650, exact. The proposal plus a mini session right after, 1 hour total, 100+ edited photos.
  • Photo + film: $2,150, exact. A second shooter joins so photos and film are captured at once.
  • Travel: none inside San Diego County.

Photos are delivered within 2 to 3 weeks. The film is a cinematic highlight of about a minute, plus vertical clips for sharing, delivered on the same timeline. Live audio of the moment, rush delivery, and same-week sneak peeks can all be added, with each fee quoted upfront, so the number you see here is the number you pay unless you ask for more.

Where can we do it?

  • Sunset Cliffs: dramatic bluffs over open ocean, the most requested sunset spot.
  • La Jolla: coves, sea cliffs, and bluff-top lawns above the water.
  • Coronado: wide, quiet sand with the skyline across the bay.
  • Mission Bay: calm water, grassy parks, and easy parking.

Anywhere in San Diego County works, with no photography travel fee inside the county.

Visiting San Diego?

Everything is planned remotely over text, email, and a free call, from the exact spot to the timing of the light, so you just land and show up. You do not need to pick a spot before you reach out: tell me your dates and I will send you two or three options with timing and parking, and one call sorts the rest.

What about weather?

If unsafe weather moves in, my photography reschedules at no charge. Your date moves, and we pick the new evening together. Traveling in with a fixed window, or a date that cannot move? Say so upfront and we build a backup slot and a rain plan into the schedule from the start.

How does booking and payment work?

One inquiry starts everything. A 50% retainer holds your date, and the balance is due 14 days before. Weather reschedules move everything to your new date. If plans shift, you can reschedule once with 30 days' notice, and a surprise that has to move on shorter notice gets my best effort to move with it. The retainer is non-refundable if you cancel outright, and if I ever have to cancel on you, you get a full refund.

Do we need a permit?

No. A proposal with the two of you and a photographer needs no permit in San Diego city parks, and that includes Sunset Cliffs. In the rare case your exact spot is an exception, I flag it while we plan and handle the paperwork for you. Bringing chairs, an arch, or a styled table into a city park is a different story: that needs a park use permit with about 15 days of lead time, so mention it early if it is part of your plan.
